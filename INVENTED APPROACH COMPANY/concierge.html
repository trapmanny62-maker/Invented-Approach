CULTURE & HISTORY

★★★★★

Kigali Genocide Memorial

The most important memorial in Rwanda. Built in Gisozi, 10 mins from city centre, it commemorates the 1994 Genocide Against the Tutsi. Over 250,000 victims are interred here in mass graves. Visitor centre has three permanent exhibitions on history leading up to genocide, managed by Aegis Trust and Kigali City Council. A place of remembrance and learning, photography inside is prohibited.

Daily 09:00-16:00 Address: KG 14 Ave, Kigali, Rwanda Contact: +250 784 651 051
★★★★☆

Inema Arts Center

Founded in 2012 by brothers Emmanuel Nkuranga and Innocent Nkurunziza, Kigali's leading contemporary art hub. Provides space for 10 artists-in-residence, gallery, and weekly traditional dance classes. Vibrant murals outside, paintings, sculptures inside, mission is to spur creativity for social and economic growth.

Daily 08:00 AM - 08:00 PM Address: KG 563 St, Kigali, Rwanda Contact: +250 783 187 646
★★★★☆

Niyo Art Gallery

Community-based gallery teaching traditional dancing, drumming, and performance to disadvantaged children and street kids. Hosts 17 artists-in-residence from Great Lakes region. Collection of paintings, sculptures, crafts, with workshop space.

Daily 8:00 AM - 11:00 PM Address: KG 572 St / KG 513 St, Gisozi, Gasabo, Kigali Contact: +250 796 607 877
★★★★☆

Kigali Convention Centre

Iconic domed landmark on KG2 Roundabout, next to Radisson Blu. Africa's Leading Conference Centre (World Travel Awards 2017). 2,600 seat auditorium, shaped like traditional Rwandan palace, lights up at night in national colors. Business hub of Kigali.

24/7 for events Address: Convention Centre Roundabout, KG2, Kimihurura, Kigali Contact: +250 2522 52252
★★★★☆

Fazenda Sengha

Equestrian and outdoor adventure park atop Mount Kigali in Nyamirambo. Horseback riding, trail rides, zip lines, archery, mountain biking, quad bikes. Panoramic views over Kigali city. Great for half-day getaway with café on site.

Tue-Sun 09:00-19:00 Address: Mount Kigali, Rwanda Contact: +250 788 551 787
★★★★☆

Rwanda Art Museum

Founded in 2009 in the former residence of President Juvénal Habyarimana (until 1994). Located 4km east of Kigali International Airport in Kanombe, Nyarugunga, Kicukiro. Now holds contemporary Rwandan and African art, plus exhibits on Rwandan history. Includes kids' art room.

Mon-Sun 08:00-18:00 Address: KK 106 St, Kigali, Rwanda Contact: +250 730 741 093
★★★★☆

Kandt House Museum (Natural History Museum)

Former home of German explorer and first colonial governor Richard Kandt (1907) on Nyarugenge Hill. Now Natural History Museum under Institute of National Museums of Rwanda. Shows evolution of life, flora/fauna of Nyungwe, Akagera, Volcanoes parks, geology of Rwanda, German-Rwanda shared history and live snakes exhibit. Best panoramic view of Mt Kigali, Mt Jali, Mt Shyorongi.

Mon-Sun 08:00-17:30 Address: KN 90 St, Nyarugenge, Kigali Contact: +250 738 650 993
★★★★★

Nyamirambo Walking Tour

Cultural walking tour run by Nyamirambo Women's Center (NWC). Takes you through Kigali's most vibrant, multicultural neighborhood — local markets, homesteads, hair salons, mosques, tailors. You get to try local snacks, learn about women's empowerment projects. Book in advance.

Tours daily 8am-7pm Address: Nyamirambo Women's Center, House 22, KN 7 Avenue, Nyarugenge Contact: +250 788 351 597

NATURE & ADVENTURE

★★★★★

Akagera National Park

Rwanda's only big 5 savanna park (1,122 km²) in Eastern Province, border Tanzania. Managed by African Parks since 2010. Restored with lions and rhinos reintroduced. Game drives, boat safari on Lake Ihema, birding (500 species). Just 2 hours from Kigali.

Gates 06:00-18:00 Address: Akagera Road, Mwiri / Kayonza, Eastern Province Contact: +250 786 182 871
★★★★★

Volcanoes National Park

The most famous park in Rwanda — part of Virunga Massif in Musanze, home to endangered mountain gorillas and golden monkeys. 160km², 5 volcanoes (Karisimbi, Bisoke etc). Gorilla trekking permit $1500, one hour with gorillas. Also Dian Fossey tomb hike.

Trekking briefing 07:00 daily Address: Kingi, Musanze, Northern Province, Rwanda Contact: +250 788 558 880
★★★★★

Nyungwe National Park — CHIMPANZEES & RAINFOREST

Largest montane rainforest in Africa (1,019 km²) in SW Rwanda bordering Burundi. Famous for Canopy Walk — 160m suspension bridge 70m above forest — chimpanzee tracking, 13 primate species, 300+ birds, waterfalls. Best biodiversity in Rwanda.

Daily 07:00-17:00 Address: Nyungwe, Bweyeye / Rangiro, Western Province Contact: +250 788 317 027
★★★★☆

Gishwati-Mukura National Park and Biosphere Reserve

Rwanda's 4th National Park (created 2015 when two forests were joined). UNESCO Biosphere Reserve in Western Province. Home to chimpanzees, golden monkeys, 232 birds, and biodiversity corridor between Nyungwe and Volcanoes. Off-the-beaten-path, for primate tracking and conservation tourism.

Daily 07:00-17:00 Address: Road, Kigeyo, Rubavu / Ngororero, Western Province Contact: Via Rwanda Development Board
★★★★☆

Mount Kigali

Not a true mountain but Kigali's highest hill (1,853m) on the west side of town. A straight-forward hike from Nyamirambo up through local homes, galleries, Fazenda Sengha, bush sections and zipline spot at the summit. Best free sunrise/sunset viewpoint over the whole city.

Open 24/7 Address: Mt. Kigali Forest, Nyarugenge, Kigali Contact: Kigali Hiking Club
★★★★★

Nyandungu Eco-Park (Nyandungu Urban Wetland Ecotourism Park)

Kigali's newest eco-park — 121 hectares of restored urban wetland in Gasabo/Kicukiro. Boardwalks, birding hides (70+ bird species), bike trails, medicinal garden, picnic lawns, restaurant. Perfect for running, family walks. One of the most rated parks in Rwanda.

Daily 06:00-21:00 Address: 24VW+Q4, Kigali, Urwibutso, Gasabo Contact: +250 796 607 788
★★★★☆

Kibuye Beach (Karongi) - Bugarama Beach

Second best beach town on Lake Kivu, between Gisenyi and Cyangugu, 135km west of Kigali. Small sandy & pebble public beaches where locals swim, known as beach resort with sunset views, kayaks, and access to Napoleon Island and Nyungwe nearby.

Daily daylight Address: Kibuye, Karongi District, Western Province Contact: Via Cormoran Lodge
★★★★☆

Gisenyi / Rubavu Public Beach - Lake Kivu

Rwanda's most famous sandy beach on Lake Kivu's NW shore. Palm-shaded, swimming, kayaking, boat to Napoleon Island. One of Great African Lakes, safe to swim (no hippos/crocs in this part). Many resorts.

Daily 07:00-19:00 Address: Avenue de la Revolution / Rubavu Public Beach, Gisenyi, Rubavu District Contact: +250 788 123 000

EVENTS

★★★★★

Umuganura — Rwanda Harvest Festival

1,000-year-old harvest thanksgiving, now a public holiday on first Friday of August. Government through Rwanda Academy of Language and Culture organizes week-long arts festivals — traditional Intore dance, exhibitions of crafts, painting, film & music, conferences. Symbolizes gratitude and sharing harvest.

First Friday in August Address: Nationwide — main national ceremony rotates
★★★★★

Car Free Day (Kigali Car Free Day)

Kigali's famous bi-monthly health day. City closes KN4 Avenue / KG 9 Ave between City Hall, Makuza Plaza, Bank of Kigali to cars from 7am-11am. Free Zumba, aerobics, yoga, cycling, medical checkups. President Kagame often joins. Held twice a month — 1st and 3rd Sunday of each month.

Every 1st & 3rd Sunday, 07:00-11:00 Kigali Car-Free Zone, KN4 Avenue / KG 9 Ave, Nyarugenge, Kigali Contact: City of Kigali
★★★★★

Tour du Rwanda

Africa's biggest cycling race, UCI 2.1, 8 stages across Land of Thousand Hills. 18th edition in 2026 covered Rukomo → Rwamagana → Nyamata → Huye → Rusizi → Karongi → Rubavu → Musanze → Kigali (22 Feb - 1 March 2026). Climbs, elevation 2,190m, international pro teams.

Annually Feb-March — 2026: Feb 22 - March 1 Starts at Amahoro Stadium prologue, finishes in Kigali Contact: Tour du Rwanda Org
★★★★★

Kigali International Peace Marathon

Africa's respected road race, now 21st edition. For first time in 2026 expanded to 2-day format June 13-14. 10km Run for Peace, Half Marathon, Full Marathon. Thousands of international runners, course records broken in 2026. Celebrates peace, unity, healthy living.

Annually in June — 2026: June 13-14 Start/Finish: Amahoro Stadium, KG 17 Avenue, Kigali Contact: +250 788 300 000
★★★★★

Umuganda Day

Rwanda's monthly national community work day — a home-grown solution. Every last Saturday of the month 08:00-11:00, everyone cleans streets, plants trees, builds. Businesses closed until 11am, traffic restricted. Embodies unity and cleanliness.

Last Saturday of every month, 08:00-11:00 Nationwide — Every village / cell Contact: Local district offices
★★★★★

Ubumuntu Arts Festival

Created 2015 by Hope Azeda (Mashirika Performing Arts), held at the Amphitheatre of Kigali Genocide Memorial. Theme: “I am because you are”. Theatre, dance, music, truth & resilience from 13+ countries. Free workshops, Ikaze Night welcome. Most emotional festival in Rwanda.

Annually in July — 12th edition: July 13-19, 2026 Amphitheatre, Kigali Genocide Memorial, KG 14 Ave, Gisozi, Gasabo, Kigali Contact: +250 788 595 545
★★★★☆

Kigali Music Festivals (Kigali Up)

Since 2011, Rwanda's biggest international music festival. Afro-fusion, reggae, hip-hop, blues. 30+ musicians from East Africa, France, Canada, plus lessons. Held at Amahoro Stadium parking lot / IPRC Kicukiro Cricket Grounds / Kigali Heights. Special editions with Nyundo Music School students.

Annually July-Aug — 2026: July 17-19 Amahoro Stadium Grounds, Gasabo, Kigali / French Institute Kimihurura Contact: +250 788 400 000
★★★★☆

Hobe Rwanda Music Festival

Since 2013, much-loved cultural fixture focused on Rwandan culture. Two-day fest in September at Serena Hotel / Petit Stade / Kigali Cultural Village. Local traditional folk musicians (Mariya Yohana, Muyango, Inganzo Ngali), dance, local art, artisan market, food tasting. Real slice of Rwandan creativity.

Annually September — 2 days Kigali Cultural Village / Kigali Serena Hotel, Kigali Contact: +250 788 123 000
★★★★★

Kwita Izina — Gorilla Naming Ceremony

Rwanda's flagship conservation event. Since 2005, names nearly 400 baby gorillas. 40 babies named in 2025 (20th edition). Global leaders, football legends, artists become namers. Celebrates community-centered conservation and gorilla population growth from 880 (2012) to 1060+ now.

Annually early September — 2026: Friday September 4 Foothills of Volcanoes National Park, Kinigi, Musanze District, Northern Province Contact: +250 788 350 000
★★★★★

Kwibuka — Genocide Commemoration & Rwanda Film Festival

Kwibuka (Remember) is Rwanda's most significant national period — 100 days of commemoration April 7-July 4 for Genocide Against Tutsi. National ceremony at Kigali Genocide Memorial hosts world leaders. Also hosts Rwanda Film Festival (Hillywood) — Africa's premier film fest in Kigali.

April 7 - July 4 annually Kigali Genocide Memorial, KG 14 Ave, Gisozi — and BK Arena for national ceremony Contact: +250 784 651 051
